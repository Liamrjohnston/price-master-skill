# Worked example

A made-up business to show the shape and depth of a good card. Not a real client.

## The brief (short version)

Sam runs a one-person garden maintenance business. He quotes $50/hr and bills eight clients monthly. Costs (van, fuel, equipment, insurance) are about $1,100 a month. He wants to take home $62,000 a year and is fully booked, turning down two enquiries a month. He wants to "put everyone up 10%" and is scared Hillside Strata will walk.

| Client | Monthly fee | Hours a month |
|---|---|---|
| Hillside Strata | $1,800 | 36 |
| Riverside Cafe | $900 | 18 |
| Patel | $660 | 12 |
| Chen | $600 | 12 |
| Okafor | $540 | 10 |
| Brooks | $540 | 11 |
| Delaney | $480 | 16 |
| Murray | $420 | 14 |

## The card

**The real problem.** **Your quoted rate is below what your business needs, and two clients pay you $30/hr.** Revenue is $5,940 a month for 129 hours (yours), a blended $46.05/hr (calc), not $50. Delaney and Murray take 30 hours (23% of your time) for $900. A flat 10% rise keeps them underpriced and still leaves you under your floor. Hillside Strata is 30% of revenue, so they get their own plan.

**Your numbers.**

| Client | Fee | Hours | Effective rate (calc) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delaney | $480 | 16 | $30.00 |
| Murray | $420 | 14 | $30.00 |
| Brooks | $540 | 11 | $49.09 |
| Hillside Strata | $1,800 | 36 | $50.00 |
| Riverside Cafe | $900 | 18 | $50.00 |
| Chen | $600 | 12 | $50.00 |
| Okafor | $540 | 10 | $54.00 |
| Patel | $660 | 12 | $55.00 |

Floor: ($62,000 take-home + $13,200 costs) x 1.10 buffer = $82,720, divided by 1,548 hours a year (129 x 12) = **$53.44/hr (calc)**. New standard rate: **$60/hr**, with room above the floor, set because you're full and turning work away.

Break-even: $50 to $60 is a 20% rise, so you could lose 0.20 / 1.20 = **16.7% of your work** and keep revenue level, before refilling the freed hours.

| Case | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Today | $5,940 | $71,280 |
| Everyone moves to $60 (129 h) | $7,740 | $92,880 |
| Delaney and Murray leave (99 h at $60) | $5,940 | $71,280, with 30 hours free to refill at $60 (+$1,800 a month) |
| Hillside also leaves (63 h at $60) | $3,780 | $45,360. This is why Hillside gets a call, not an email |

**Who gets what, when.**

| Who | New price | From | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| New enquiries | $60/hr | Now | Quotes |
| Riverside, Patel, Chen, Okafor, Brooks | $60/hr on current hours | 1 March (about 60 days' notice) | Message |
| Delaney, Murray | $60/hr ($960, $840), or a smaller visit that fits their budget | 1 March | Message with a clean way out |
| Hillside Strata | $55/hr ($1,980) at the March renewal, $60/hr ($2,160) from September | Agreed on the call | Call this week, then email |

**Send these.**

Standard (Riverside Cafe shown):
> Hi [name], thanks for having me look after the cafe garden. Every visit I mow, edge, weed the beds and keep the patio planters going. I'm fully booked and turning work away, and my prices haven't kept up with that, which is on me. From 1 March my rate will be $60 an hour, so your monthly fee goes from $900 to $1,080, about $42 a week more. You'll still get me on the same day every week. Happy to talk it through any time. Thanks, Sam

Delaney and Murray:
> Hi [name], from 1 March my rate will be $60 an hour, which makes your monthly visit $[new]. If you'd rather keep the cost nearer where it is, I can do a shorter visit covering [the essentials] for $[price]. And if neither suits, that's completely fine; I'll leave you notes on the garden for whoever takes over. Thanks for having me.

Hillside Strata (book the call):
> Hi [name], I'm setting next year's pricing and want to talk it through with you before your renewal. Could we find 20 minutes this week?

On the call: what the contract now covers, the two-step price with dates, and that the crew and schedule stay the same.

**If they push back.**
- Too expensive: "I understand. If the full visit is more than you want, I can do [shorter visit] for [price]."
- Keep my old rate: "I'm moving everyone to the same rate so it's fair to all my clients."
- Someone's cheaper: "That's fair. What I give you is the same person every visit, on the same day. If price is the main thing, I understand."
- Silence after 1 March: invoice at the new rate with "First invoice at the new rate we talked about."

**Do today.**
1. Quote $60/hr to the next enquiry.
2. Book the Hillside call.
3. Send the standard and Delaney/Murray messages this week.
4. Put 1 March in your invoicing.

**Check these.** Hillside's contract notice terms. Whether the hours include travel. Whether $62,000 is before or after tax.

Note that the reason in the message (fully booked, turning work away) comes straight from Sam's brief. Note what the card does not say: no "the going rate for gardeners is", no "most clients won't mind", no deadline, and no promise that nobody leaves.
